Code Office Publisher는 유튜브 채널 “임박사의 은밀한 사생활”의 소유자가 자신의 채널에 영상을 올리고 관리하기 위해 직접 만든 개인용 도구입니다. 오피스 웹툰 「코드오피스」 홍보 쇼츠와 정주행 영상을 업로드하고, 제목·설명·썸네일·댓글을 관리하는 데 사용합니다.

Code Office Publisher is a personal tool built and used only by the owner of the YouTube channel “임박사의 은밀한 사생활” to upload videos to that channel and manage their titles, descriptions, thumbnails and comments via the YouTube Data API. It is not offered to other users.

What it does

  • Uploads the channel owner's own videos (videos.insert) and sets their thumbnails.
  • Updates titles and descriptions of the owner's own videos (videos.update).
  • Posts comments as the channel on the owner's own videos (commentThreads.insert).
  • Reads the owner's own channel and video information to confirm the target channel.

This tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See our Privacy Policy.
