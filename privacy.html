1. Who uses this app

Code Office Publisher is used solely by the owner of the YouTube channel “임박사의 은밀한 사생활” to manage that channel. It does not provide sign-in to, or collect data from, any other person.

이 앱은 채널 소유자 본인만 사용하며, 다른 사람의 데이터를 수집하지 않습니다.

2. Data accessed through YouTube API Services

With the owner's authorization (Google OAuth, scopes youtube.upload and youtube.force-ssl), the app accesses only the owner's own YouTube data needed for publishing: channel ID and title, the owner's video metadata (titles, descriptions, tags, privacy status, thumbnails) and comments the owner posts on their own videos.

3. How the data is used

The data is used only to upload and manage the owner's own videos and comments on the owner's channel. It is not used for advertising, analytics profiling, or any other purpose.

4. Storage and sharing

OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's personal computer with owner-only file permissions. The app has no server and does not store, sell, transfer or share any user data with third parties. No cookies or tracking are used on this website.

인증 토큰은 소유자의 개인 컴퓨터에만 저장되며, 어떤 데이터도 제3자에게 판매·전송·공유하지 않습니다.

5. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only while the owner uses the tool and can be deleted at any time by removing the local token file. Access can also be revoked at any time from the Google Account security settings (third-party access).

6. Google and YouTube policies

This app uses YouTube API Services. Use of YouTube data is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
